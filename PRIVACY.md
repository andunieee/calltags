# CallTags privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-05_

CallTags is a phone (dialer) app. This policy explains what it does with your data.

## What the app accesses

- **Call log.** To show your call history, CallTags reads the phone's call log (numbers, dates, durations, call type and the cached contact name, if any) and keeps a copy in a database inside the app.
- **Labels.** The labels you add to calls are stored in that same database.
- **Phone and calls.** As your default phone app, CallTags places, answers and manages calls through Android's telephony system. It uses the microphone only as part of a phone call, the way any phone app does.

## What leaves your device

Nothing. CallTags has no account, no servers, no analytics, no ads and does not request internet access. Your call history and labels never leave your phone through the app.

If you have Android's own device backup turned on, Android may include the app's data in your device backup, as it does for other apps. That is handled by Android and your backup provider, not by CallTags.

## Deleting your data

Remove a call and its labels from the app's history, or uninstall the app to delete everything it stored.
